Experiences that open new pathways

We design gatherings where movement, music, and conversation weave together. Each experience is crafted to help communities regulate collectively, express freely, and leave with practices they can carry home.

Live Immersive Sessions

Multi-sensory sessions that blend facilitated dialogue, breathwork, and collaborative art-making. We focus on creating brave spaces where participants can experiment with new ways of relating to themselves and each other.

  • Intention setting, grounding, and integration moments built into every agenda.
  • Live music and somatic cues that help the room move together.
  • Take-home practices so the resonance continues after the event.

Retreats & Residencies

Small, intentional gatherings for artists and organizers who want to reset. We curate daily rhythms that balance rest, creative play, and nervous system care in supportive environments.

  • Morning regulation labs to anchor the group before collaborative work.
  • Creative studios with gentle facilitation and resource libraries.
  • Integration circles that honor personal stories and collective learning.

Workshops & Talks

Actionable education for teams, festivals, and classrooms. Every session includes a blend of science-backed insight and embodied practice so participants can apply what they learn immediately.

  • Topics include creative resilience, trauma-informed collaboration, and somatic first aid.
  • Customizable agendas to meet the needs of artists, educators, and organizers.
  • Resource sheets and recordings for continued practice after the workshop.

Community Care Spaces

Drop-in circles, virtual lounges, and listening sessions where creatives can be witnessed without performance pressure. These gatherings are designed to cultivate collective resilience and care.

  • Low-barrier formats that welcome all experience levels.
  • Peer witnessing practices rooted in consent and curiosity.
  • Follow-up notes with invitations for ongoing connection.

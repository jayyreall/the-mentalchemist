Out of the Shadow Artist Retreat (May 2027)

You are an artist. The only thing in your way is your own voice, convincing you that you’re not ready, not good enough, or not “official” yet. That voice kept you safe once, but now it’s holding you back. This retreat is your turning point. The moment you step out of your own shadow, embrace it, and claim what’s already yours. We've carefully curated a one weekend experience to guide you from “I want to be” to “I am”, and with tangible evidence that even your inner critic can't deny.

Honor your inner inspiration as you spend the weekend in stunning San Juan, Puerto Rico, letting your creativity breathe, stretch, and expand. Whether you paint, dance, sing, shoot, write, or blend mediums, you’re welcome here. Once you say yes to yourself, we take care of the rest: flights, lodging, workshops, group meals, and once-in-a-lifetime experiences with a small group of artists who understand both the struggle and the calling.

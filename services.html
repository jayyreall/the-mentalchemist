Digital and In-Person Services

The Mentalchemist Match

  • Who is it for: For people who know they need real support but feel overwhelmed, discouraged, or unsure where to start when seeking aligned practitioners, healers, or collaborators.
  • What does it do: Connects you to therapists, bodyworkers, healers, and creatives who match your needs, identity, nervous system, and values. Removing the guesswork and the emotional labor of searching alone.
  • How your life shifts: You get care that fits. Your healing accelerates because you’re supported by people who actually understand you and meet you where you are.
  • How much is it: Pay-what-you-can / donation-based.

Somatic Roadmap and Alignment Sequence

  • Who is it for: For people who feel stuck in survival mode. The chronically dysregulated, drained, overwhelmed, or out of sync with themselves. If you’ve been seeking a clear, embodied path back to stability and direction, this is aligned with your needs.
  • What does it do: Reveals your stress patterns, maps where your body holds tension and resilience, and guides you through a structured recalibration process that resets your system and clarifies your next steps.
  • How your life shifts: Your reactions make sense. You shift out of autopilot. Your body becomes responsive instead of reactive. You move through your days with more grounding, clearer instincts, and a sense of forward motion towards consistency.
  • How much is it: $22 for the personalized foundational roadmap; $222 per alignment session with personalized adjustments and follow-up practices.

Creative Embodiment

  • Who is it for: For creative individuals who feel blocked, under-supported, or disconnected from their creative identity, and need guidance that treats their artistry and their humanity with equal weight.
  • What does it do: Provides direct mentorship backed by tailored digital tools and individualized roadmaps to refine your technique, strengthen your artistic voice, and support performance readiness with emotional grounding.
  • How your life shifts: Your sound sharpens, your confidence stabilizes, and your art finally reflects what you’ve been trying to express. You stop hiding in your craft and start taking up your rightful creative space.
  • How much is it: $222 per 1:1 session; Digital tools, single offerings, and package pricing vary based on the level of support you choose.

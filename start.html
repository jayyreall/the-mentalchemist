How to prepare

  • Check in with your body: Notice your energy level, nervous system cues, and how much time you can honestly give yourself right now.
  • Choose one next step: Pick a single resource—product, service, or experience—and commit to trying it before adding more.
  • Set a simple ritual: Create a small pre-session routine (water, breath, stretch) to signal safety and presence.

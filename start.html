Where To Start

Not everyone enters this work the same way. To make it easier, here are three suggested paths based on how you naturally move through growth. Whether you want clear guidance from the start or prefer to explore at your own pace, there’s a path that matches your capacity and goals.

Find your starting place

Clarity Flow: Intentional Direction → Practical Integration

Best for people who feel overwhelmed, confused about where to start, or unsure what they “need.”

  • Step 1
    • The Mentalchemist Match

    Quick intake that identifies the right entry point based on goals, capacity, and current patterns.

  • Step 2
    • One-Time Somatic Consult + Personalized Roadmap

    A diagnostic session that maps out what’s happening in your body, what’s driving the patterns, and areas of focus to facilitate shifts.

  • Step 3
    • >Alignment Sequence

    Use the roadmap to create sustained change through the Mentalchemy framework: Awareness → Embodiment → Alchemy → Integration.

  • Step 4
    • Digital Tools (Workbooks, Journals, Guides)

    Supplement between sessions for accountability, deeper reflection, and self-paced support.

Self-paced Flow: Grounded Support → Experiential Integration

Best for people who like to explore independently first or require the flexibility of self-paced engagement.

  • Step 1
    • Digital Tools (Workbooks, Journals, Guides)

    Build foundational awareness, explore patterns, and get a feel for your style and approach.

  • Step 2
    • The Mentalchemist Match

    Use insights from the digital tools to identify the next best step tailored to your readiness.

  • Step 3
    • One-Time Somatic Consult + Personalized Roadmap

    Grounded assessment, somatic mapping, and a personalized plan without committing to long-term coaching.

  • Step 4
    • Immersive Healing Experiences

    Participate in local events centered in communal learning, deeper embodiment, and immersive transformation.

Artist Flow: Creative Unblocking → Embodied Expression

Best for artists, performers, and creatives who want to reconnect with their voice, unblock their creative channels, or rebuild trust with their artistic identity.

  • Step 1
    • Digital Tools (Workbooks, Journals, Guides)

    Start with low-pressure exercises, reflection prompts, or creative-focused workbooks to surface patterns around expression, inhibition, and capacity.

  • Step 2
    • The Mentalchemist Match

    A tailored intake that identifies whether your next step should be artistry coaching, somatic work, or a creative-focused immersive experience.

  • Step 3
    • Artistry Development

    Focused sessions that blend somatics, performance coaching, expressive work, and identity reclamation. Ideal for rebuilding trust with your creative voice, strengthening stage presence, and unlocking embodied expression.

  • Step 4
    • Immersive Healing Experiences

    Move into creative environments designed for deeper expression. Spaces that blend somatics, creativity, and community to support your artistic expansion and reconnection with your craft.

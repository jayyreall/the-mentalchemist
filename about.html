Who We Are

The Mentalchemist is a holistic studio supporting musicians and creative practitioners through trauma-informed, somatic, and artistic care. Our mission is to make healing practical, inclusive, and rooted in lived experience. Our vision is a world where emotional literacy, body awareness, and liberation are considered essential skills and abilities, not luxuries. We honor values of liberation, embodiment, autonomy, transformation, truth, accessibility, decolonization, creativity, compassion, and integrity; not as hierarchy, but as an interactive web of what it means to be fully human.

Values That Guide Us

  • Compassion-first relationships that honor personal stories.
  • Equity-minded services for artists across identities and genres.
  • Curiosity-driven experimentation that keeps creativity vibrant.

Our Integrative Approach

We combine evidence-backed wellbeing strategies with deep industry experience to cultivate sustainable, creative lives. We blend somatic awareness, personal coaching, and community-informed practices, to collaborate with each client in a manner that aligns wtih their wants, needs, and ambitions. When you engage with The Mentalchemist, you’re not entering a program; you’re joining a movement that believes healing is a right, not a privilege.
